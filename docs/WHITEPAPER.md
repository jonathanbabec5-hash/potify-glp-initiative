# Green Light Pay Initiative
## Whitepaper · public developer edition

**November 25, 2026 · Public microsite source**

### Goal and audience

Recruit operators who can identify concrete friction at the counter. The immediate audience is retail operators and merchant-service partners.

### Design and implementation

A static public initiative site explains the research and simulates checkout. It depends on a separate approved provider for any production payment or funding route. A public demonstration gives an operator a common screen to discuss. The approval chain and legal entities still have to be established outside the demo.

### What a contributor can establish

The public entry page returned HTTP 200 and rendered local content in Chromium at a 390 × 844 viewport. Record interview findings and proposed integrations separately. A contact who volunteers for an interview is not an approved processor.

### Evidence and outstanding work

No provider clearance or funded deployment is inferred. Historical policy copy requires a fresh review before a new campaign. The next acceptance case is concrete. A visitor can follow the fictional QR example and distinguish a simulation from a settled receipt.

### Place in POTIFY

Use interview evidence to scope one independently contracted commercial pilot. Keep sponsorship, research contributions and sales separate. The marketing plan gives the test window and the evidence for a commercial outcome.
