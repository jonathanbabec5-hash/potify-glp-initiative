The Virginia story

Opening day is only the beginning.

A retail license can open a shop. Taking a digital payment takes a provider willing to work with that licensed business. This project starts at that counter.

July 2027 · State timeline

Virginia is building a licensed retail market.

The Cannabis Control Authority lists July 1, 2027 as the start of adult-use retail sales. The published schedule anticipates finalized rules in December 2026 and initial licensing milestones in spring 2027.

Read the CCA timeline ↗

State law · Banking

Virginia law gives banks room to serve licensed shops.

Virginia Code § 6.2-108 permits banks and credit unions to provide services including payment processing, subject to applicable state and federal law. Institutions choose whether to participate and conduct due diligence.

Read Virginia Code § 6.2-108 ↗

Federal guidance · Due diligence

Payment providers have obligations of their own.

FinCEN’s guidance covers due diligence and reporting for financial institutions serving marijuana-related businesses. We’re looking for written approval of the exact Virginia use case from a provider and its banking partner.

Read FinCEN’s guidance ↗

Nov 13, 2026 · Published

Virginia research and the first public simulation

We traced the retail timeline and banking rules to primary sources and built a sample checkout that anyone can try.

In design

A private receipt and portable payment integration

The proposed QR carries a short-lived checkout reference. A verified provider would handle authorization and settlement. Customers and their merchants would see their own records, with provider and legally required access where applicable. Personal data stays off public chains.

Next milestone

Evidence from merchants and providers

We’ll ask merchants about checkout delays, pricing and refunds, and ask providers to document what they can support in Virginia. A pilot depends on written approval, licensed participants, and successful testing.
